SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

Sources: CBRT EVDS (TLREF, CBRT average funding cost, BIST repo, indicative prices of government securities, Survey of Market Participants, reserves, non-resident securities holdings, open market operations, BIS policy rates) and the Borsa connector (FX, gold, Brent, crypto, BIST and US prices, CPI, technical indicators, economic calendar). TLREF is published after 16:00 Istanbul time.

Approximate OIS: for a discount bill with d days to maturity and indicative price P, OIS-equivalent fixed rate = (100/P − 1) × 365/d; implied average overnight rate = 365 × ((100/P)^(1/d) − 1). Not an actual TLREF OIS quote. Bond yields are solved from coupon-inclusive indicative prices; the curve is a Nelson–Siegel fit. Gold per ounce is derived from gram gold and USD/TRY (31.1035 g); FX crosses are computed from TRY rates.

The page refreshes automatically several times each weekday; an open tab reloads itself every 30 minutes.
