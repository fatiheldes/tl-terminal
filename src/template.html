FMON 1Equity index futures vs cashfront quarterly contract · basis and implied carry

FutureContractFuturesCash indexBasisBasis %To expiryImplied carry p.a.3M T-billT-bill − carry ≈ div. yieldCheck

Implied carry = (futures ÷ cash − 1) × 360 ÷ days to the third Friday of the contract month. Fair value is cash × (1 + (financing − dividends) × days/360), so T-bill minus implied carry approximates the dividend yield the market prices. Futures trade almost round the clock while cash indices only print in exchange hours, so the basis is distorted when the two prices are taken at different times (e.g. US holidays).

FMON 2FX futures vs spotCME front contract · implied USD − foreign rate gap

FuturePairFuturesSpotPointsTo expiryImplied gap p.a.Policy gapCheck

By covered interest parity, futures ÷ spot − 1 ≈ (USD rate − foreign rate) × days/360. Policy gap = Fed rate minus the foreign central bank rate (BIS). With few days left to expiry, small price differences move the implied gap a lot.

Approximate OIS: for a discount bill with d days to maturity and indicative price P, OIS-equivalent fixed rate = (100/P − 1) × 365/d; implied average overnight rate = 365 × ((100/P)^(1/d) − 1). Not an actual TLREF OIS quote. Bond yields are solved from coupon-inclusive indicative prices; the curve is a Nelson–Siegel fit. Gold per ounce is derived from gram gold and USD/TRY (31.1035 g); FX crosses are computed from TRY rates.
